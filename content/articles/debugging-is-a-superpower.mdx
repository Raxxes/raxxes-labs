# Debugging Is a Superpower

Every developer encounters bugs. The difference is not that experienced developers never make mistakes. They have learned how to investigate problems systematically.

## Read the Error Carefully

When something breaks, resist the urge to change several things at once.

Read the error message. Identify the relevant file, line number, and operation. The message may not reveal the complete cause, but it often gives you a useful starting point.

## Change One Thing at a Time

Try to reproduce the problem consistently. Form a possible explanation, make one focused change, and observe the result.

Changing multiple things simultaneously makes it difficult to identify which change actually fixed the issue.

## Learn From Every Bug

After solving a problem, record what caused it and why the fix worked.

Over time, these notes become a personal troubleshooting guide. Problems that once seemed confusing become easier to recognize and solve.

Debugging is not wasted time. It is part of learning how software actually works.
